# Lehmann & Romano, 2005 — Testing Statistical Hypotheses (3rd ed.)

**Authors:** E. L. Lehmann and Joseph P. Romano
**Year:** 2005 (3rd edition)
**Venue:** Springer (Springer Texts in Statistics)
**ISBN:** 978-0387988641

## One-paragraph summary
The canonical graduate-level reference for classical hypothesis testing
("TSH"), originally written by E. L. Lehmann in 1959 and substantially
expanded by Romano for the 3rd edition. Covers the Neyman–Pearson lemma,
UMP and UMP-unbiased tests, exponential families with monotone
likelihood ratio, ancillary statistics and conditional inference, and
the Kolmogorov–Smirnov statistic. Standard reference for both the
exponential-family setup in §5.1 (D-EF) and the Kolmogorov limit of
the KS statistic invoked in §7.3.

## Why CD-SBI cites it
- §5.1 D-EF: standard textbook treatment of regular one-parameter
  exponential families with monotone likelihood ratio in a continuous
  sufficient statistic.
- §7.3 KS noise floor: the Kolmogorov limiting distribution
  `Pr(√N · KS_N > 1.628) ≈ 0.01` for a fully-specified reference
  distribution.

## Specific anchors
- §3.4 — exponential families and MLR.
- §4.4 — UMP-unbiased tests in exponential families (background for
  the Schweder–Hjort UMPU CD bridge).
- §14.2 — Kolmogorov–Smirnov tests and the asymptotic Kolmogorov
  distribution.

## Notes
- The 4th edition (2022, with Romano) covers most of the same material
  with updated notation and additional sections; the 3rd edition is
  more commonly cited.
- An alternative for the exp-family material is Brown 1986
  (*Fundamentals of Statistical Exponential Families*, IMS Lecture
  Notes), which is more specialized.
