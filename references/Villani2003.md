# Villani, 2003 — Topics in Optimal Transportation

**Authors:** Cédric Villani
**Year:** 2003
**Venue:** American Mathematical Society, *Graduate Studies in Mathematics* vol. 58
**ISBN:** 978-0821833124

## One-paragraph summary
The first of Villani's two textbooks on optimal transport (the longer
*Optimal Transport: Old and New*, 2009, came later). Covers the
Monge–Kantorovich problem, Brenier's polar factorization, the
one-dimensional monotone-rearrangement theorem (between two atomless
probability measures on `ℝ` there is a unique increasing measure-
preserving map, given by the composition of the target CDF with the
inverse source CDF), and the Knothe–Rosenblatt rearrangement in higher
dimensions. Standard graduate-level reference for the OT machinery
that underlies §4.2's Lemma 4.1 and §6.3's KR construction.

## Why CD-SBI cites it
- §4.2 Lemma 4.1 (monotone transport): the classical 1D monotone-
  rearrangement uniqueness result. Villani 2003 ch.~2 is the standard
  textbook treatment.

## Specific anchors
- Theorem 2.5 / §2.1 — 1D monotone-rearrangement uniqueness between
  atomless probability measures on `ℝ`.
- §2.18 — Knothe–Rosenblatt rearrangement (briefly; the longer
  treatment is in *Optimal Transport: Old and New*, 2009, ch.~1).

## Notes
- Villani's 2009 sequel (*Optimal Transport: Old and New*, Grundlehren
  vol. 338, Springer) is the more comprehensive reference but is
  significantly longer; for the 1D / KR pieces CD-SBI needs, the 2003
  book is enough.
- Alternative textbook references: Santambrogio 2015 (*Optimal Transport
  for Applied Mathematicians*, Springer) covers similar ground and is
  often easier to navigate; Ambrosio–Gigli–Savaré 2008 is the
  metric-measure-space reference.
