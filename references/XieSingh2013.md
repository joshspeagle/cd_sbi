# Xie & Singh, 2013 — Confidence distribution, the frequentist distribution estimator of a parameter: A review

**Authors:** M. Xie, K. Singh
**Year:** 2013
**Venue:** International Statistical Review, vol. 81, no. 1, pp. 3–39 (with discussion)
**arXiv:** n/a (preprint on Rutgers stat website)
**DOI:** 10.1111/insr.12000

## One-paragraph summary
The canonical *review article* on confidence distributions, written as a survey of the modern frequentist CD framework. Covers the definition (a calibrated data-dependent CDF on the parameter space), connections to fiducial inference, bootstrap distributions, significance functions, and normalized likelihood. Shows how CDs unify a wide range of frequentist constructions — confidence intervals, p-value functions, profile likelihoods — under one umbrella, and surveys applications to meta-analysis, model selection, and prediction. Often cited together with Singh–Xie–Strawderman 2007 as the pair of papers that made the modern CD framework accessible to applied statisticians; followed by a multi-discussant exchange.
