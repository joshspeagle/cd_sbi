# Stevens, 1950 — Fiducial limits of the parameter of a discontinuous distribution

**Authors:** W. L. Stevens
**Year:** 1950
**Venue:** *Biometrika*, 37(1/2), 117–129
**DOI:** 10.1093/biomet/37.1-2.117

## One-paragraph summary
Early paper in the fiducial-inference tradition that introduces the
auxiliary-randomization device for parameters of discrete distributions.
Stevens proposes adding an independent `U ~ U(0,1)` to spread each atom
of a discrete sufficient statistic into a continuous interval — exactly
the construction the manuscript calls the *randomized PIT* at §5.7.1.
The formula `V_θ(X, U) = F_θ(T(X)^-) + U · p_θ(T(X))` is essentially
Stevens's construction. Tocher 1950 (in the same volume of *Biometrika*)
independently proposes the same device.

## Why CD-SBI cites it
- §5.7.1 D-randPIT: the priority for the randomized-PIT formula
  belongs to Stevens 1950 (and Tocher 1950 independently). Lancaster
  1961, currently cited alone, is the more common modern reference for
  the construction and is also where the mid-p value is formally
  introduced.

## Specific anchors
- Construction of fiducial limits for discrete distributions: §2 of
  Stevens 1950, pp. 117–119.

## Notes
- Tocher 1950 ("Extension of the Neyman–Pearson theory of tests to
  discontinuous variates", *Biometrika* 37, 130–144) is an
  independent contemporaneous reference for the same auxiliary
  randomization. CD-SBI cites Lancaster 1961 as the primary modern
  source and Stevens 1950 as the priority reference.
