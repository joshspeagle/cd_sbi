# Cranmer, Brehmer & Louppe, 2020 — The frontier of simulation-based inference

**Authors:** K. Cranmer, J. Brehmer, G. Louppe
**Year:** 2020
**Venue:** Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS), vol. 117, no. 48, pp. 30055–30062
**arXiv:** 1911.01429
**DOI:** 10.1073/pnas.1912789117

## One-paragraph summary
The canonical PNAS review article on simulation-based inference. Surveys the modern SBI landscape — ABC, neural posterior / likelihood / ratio estimation, active learning, embedding networks, sequential schemes — and frames SBI as the deep-learning-era successor to classical ABC. Argues that the field's progress is being driven by three threads: improved expressiveness from deep networks, better sample efficiency from sequential / active strategies, and growing scientific demand for likelihood-free analysis in physics, biology, and cosmology. Standard reference for the broader SBI context that CD-SBI is positioning itself within.
