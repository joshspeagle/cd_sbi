# Lyons, 2013 — Distance covariance in metric spaces

**Authors:** R. Lyons
**Year:** 2013
**Venue:** Annals of Probability, vol. 41, no. 5, pp. 3284–3305
**arXiv:** 1106.5758
**DOI:** 10.1214/12-AOP803

## One-paragraph summary
Extends Székely–Rizzo distance covariance from Euclidean to arbitrary metric spaces of *negative type*. The original distance-covariance theory was Fourier-based and Euclidean-specific; Lyons gives a clean construction via metric embeddings into Hilbert spaces, characterizes when the distance covariance equals zero iff random variables are independent (the "characteristic" property), and connects the construction to RKHS embeddings of probability measures. The headline technical result is that on a separable metric space of strong negative type, distance covariance characterizes joint independence — which is the foundational fact used by HSIC / kernel-based independence tests.
