# Singh, Xie & Strawderman, 2007 — Confidence distribution (CD) — distribution estimator of a parameter

**Authors:** K. Singh, M. Xie, W. E. Strawderman
**Year:** 2007
**Venue:** IMS Lecture Notes — Monograph Series, vol. 54 ("Complex Datasets and Inverse Problems"), pp. 132–150
**arXiv:** 0708.0976
**DOI:** 10.1214/074921707000000102

## One-paragraph summary
The paper that re-introduced "confidence distribution" as a first-class frequentist concept in the modern literature. Defines a CD as a function `H(·; X)` of the data that is, for each X, a CDF on Θ and that is calibrated in the sense `H(θ_0; X) ~ U(0,1)` under `X ~ P_{θ_0}`. Develops information measures, comparisons, and notions of *optimal* CD (proto-versions of what Schweder–Hjort later canonicalized). Connects the CD concept to the profile likelihood and discusses multi-parameter generalizations, fiducial intervals, and bootstrap CDs. Together with Xie–Singh 2013 it is the entry point into the modern CD literature from the statistics side.
